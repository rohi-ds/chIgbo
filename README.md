# chIgbo

Learn Igbo, the language of the Igbo people of south-eastern Nigeria — three
words a day.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static host
(`python3 -m http.server`). There is no build step and no backend; progress is
stored in the browser (`localStorage`) and can be exported from **Progress**.

## What is in it

- **Today** – three new words every day, core vocabulary first, with a tone
  melody you can see and hear.
- **Daily quiz** – 15 multiple-choice questions: today's words, words that are
  due for review, and new ones. Keyboard: `1`–`4` to answer, `Enter` for next.
- **Practice** – spaced-repetition flashcards, a pair-matching game, a typing
  drill and unlimited extra quiz rounds.
- **Dictionary** – 8,079 entries, searchable in Igbo (dots and accents
  optional) or English, filterable by topic, letter and part of speech. Add
  your own words and they join the dictionary and the practice deck.
- **Progress** – streak, XP, activity heatmap, collection strength, settings.

## Dictionary data

`data/dictionary.js` is generated by `tools/build_dictionary.py` from Kay
Williamson's *Dictionary of Ònìchà Igbo* (2nd edition, edited by Roger Blench,
2006) in the JSON digitisation published by the
[Igbo API](https://github.com/nkowaokwu/igbo_api) project under the Apache 2.0
licence (copy and licence in `tools/source/`).

Things to know about the data:

- It records **Onitsha Igbo**, so some words differ from Standard Igbo
  (`ụno` for `ụlọ` "house", `mmili` for `mmiri` "water").
- The source marks tone on every word (grave = low, macron = step-down,
  unmarked = high). The script turns those accents into a tone melody and shows
  the word in everyday spelling without them; every entry keeps the
  tone-marked form.
- Verbs are listed as bare roots (`li` "to eat"), as in the source.
- The digitisation was automatic and is noisy: some definitions and example
  sentences are attached to the wrong headword. The script drops rows it
  cannot use and keeps only example sentences that contain the headword.
  Correct individual entries in `tools/fixes.json`.

To leave words out, add them to `tools/exclude.txt` and rebuild:

    python3 tools/build_dictionary.py
